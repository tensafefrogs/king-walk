Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026. This covers King Walk on the web and the King Walk app for iPhone and iPad.

King Walk has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases, and collects nothing that identifies you.

Your progress

Your puzzle rating, history and settings are saved on your device. In the iOS app, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so your progress follows you to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer can't see this copy; Apple stores it under its own privacy policy.

The home screen widget reads the same progress from the app on your device. Nothing leaves your device for it.

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your device. The reminder doesn't go through any server, and you can turn it off in the game's settings or in the Settings app.

To delete your progress: Menu → Start over erases it on this device, and in the app also its iCloud copy. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it from that device.

Game Center

In the iOS app, if you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your puzzle rating and best streak to the King Walk leaderboards, and unlocks achievements as you play. Game Center is run by Apple: what other players can see depends on your Game Center settings, and Apple handles it under its own privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app, and the game works the same without it.

Anonymous play counts

The game counts anonymous events, for example how many people start, how many reach 10 or 100 puzzles, which rank they reach, and which kind of device they play on (such as iPhone, iPad, Android or a computer). It uses GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies, doesn't store IP addresses (it uses them only to estimate the country) and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

The counts are totals, not records about a person, so there is nothing that could be linked to you, looked up or deleted for you. They aren't shared or sold. If you'd rather not send them, play offline: nothing is sent then, and content blockers stop them in a browser.

Links to other sites

Each puzzle links to its page on Lichess, where the puzzle comes from. Links open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

King Walk is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will say so and show a new date. Questions: @tensafefrogs on X.
