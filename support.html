King Walk Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: post to @tensafefrogs on X, or open an issue on GitHub.

How do I play?

Each puzzle starts with the other side's move. Then it is your turn: tap a piece, then tap where it goes, or drag it. Open the menu (top right) → How it works for the details.

Why did my rating go down?

A miss, or showing the answer, lowers your rating a little and makes the next puzzles easier. A clean solve raises it. A solve with a hint counts as half.

How do I move my progress to another device?

In the iOS app, progress is copied to your iCloud account and appears on your other iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. To move it between the app and the website, or between browsers, use Menu → Advanced → Copy my save code, then paste it on the other device and press Load.

The daily puzzle

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, and a new one starts at midnight. Monday's is gentle and each day gets a little harder, up to Sunday. Hints are fine; showing the answer ends your daily streak. It doesn't change your rating.

Streak freezes

Every 7 days in a row earns a streak freeze (you can hold 2). If you skip a day, one is used and your daily streak carries on.

Daily reminder

In the iOS app: Menu → Daily reminder, then pick a time. You get one notification a day, and none once that day's puzzle is solved. Choose Off to stop them.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app to see the leaderboards and achievements (Menu → Game Center in the app). The game works the same without it.

Widgets, Control Center and Siri

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and pick King Walk: one widget shows your rank and rating, the other today's puzzle and your daily streak (it also works in StandBy and on the Lock Screen). On iOS 18 and later you can add a Daily puzzle button to Control Center. Or say "Play today's King Walk puzzle" to Siri, or touch and hold the app icon.

How do I start over?

Menu → Start over, then tap it again to confirm. This erases your progress.
